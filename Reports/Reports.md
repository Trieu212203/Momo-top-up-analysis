# Monthly Performance – Insight Analysis

# Dashboard Insight Analysis

![Dashboard](Dashboard/Monthly_performance.png)



## 1. Executive Summary

This dashboard analyzes the monthly performance of the platform using key business metrics including **GMV, revenue, active users, transactions, AOV, and ARPU**.

Key observations show that the platform experienced **steady growth in users and transaction volume throughout the year**, which significantly contributed to revenue growth. However, **Average Order Value (AOV) shows a slight decline**, suggesting smaller purchase sizes per transaction.

Overall, business growth appears to be driven primarily by **increasing user activity rather than higher spending per order**.

---

# 2. Revenue Performance

### GMV and Revenue Trend

* Total GMV reached **694.6M**
* Total revenue generated was **18.7M**

Revenue and GMV follow a **generally upward trend throughout the year**, with noticeable peaks around **September to October**.

#### Key observations

* Early months show moderate fluctuations in revenue.
* Revenue begins to stabilize and grow steadily after **May**.
* The highest revenue values occur during **Q3 and early Q4**, suggesting potential seasonal demand or successful marketing campaigns.

#### Insight

The increase in revenue appears to correlate strongly with **growth in transactions and active users**, indicating that business expansion is driven by increased platform usage.

---

# 3. User Activity Analysis

### Active Users Trend

The number of active users increases consistently over time.

* January: ~1000 users
* December: ~1234 users

This represents a **steady growth in user engagement across the year**.

#### Key observations

* A temporary drop occurs around **April**, but growth resumes afterward.
* Strong user growth is observed from **July onward**.

#### Insight

The platform demonstrates **successful user acquisition and retention strategies**, contributing to overall transaction growth.

---

# 4. Transaction Trend

Total transactions show a clear upward trajectory over the year.

* Early months fluctuate slightly.
* From **May onward**, transactions grow steadily.
* **December records the highest transaction volume (~1255).**

#### Insight

Transaction growth aligns closely with the **increase in active users**, suggesting that newly acquired users are actively engaging with the platform rather than remaining passive.

This indicates **healthy user engagement and repeat purchasing behavior**.

---

# 5. Unit Economics Analysis

### AOV and ARPU Trends

Two key monetization metrics are analyzed:

* **AOV (Average Order Value): 51,568**
* **ARPU (Average Revenue Per User): 1,392**

#### Observations

* **AOV gradually declines throughout the year**, indicating smaller order sizes.
* **ARPU remains relatively stable**, fluctuating around the 1300 range.

#### Insight

The divergence between AOV and ARPU suggests:

* Users may be making **more frequent purchases**
* But each individual purchase may involve **lower spending**

This indicates a shift toward **higher transaction frequency rather than larger basket sizes**.

---

# 6. Monthly Revenue Dynamics

Revenue Month-over-Month (MoM) fluctuations provide additional insights.

### Notable changes

* **March:** strong revenue growth (+14.91%)
* **June:** another strong increase (+10.49%)
* **September:** continued positive growth (+5.16%)
* **November:** noticeable decline (-8.97%)

#### Insight

Revenue volatility suggests that **external factors such as promotions, campaigns, or seasonality** may play a significant role in monthly performance.

---

# 7. Key Business Insights

From the overall analysis, several important insights emerge:

### 1. Growth is driven by user activity

The increase in **active users and transactions** is the primary driver of revenue growth.

### 2. Monetization per order is decreasing

The declining AOV suggests that **average spending per transaction is decreasing**.

### 3. Engagement is improving

Despite lower order values, **users are interacting with the platform more frequently**.

### 4. Seasonal effects exist

Revenue peaks during **Q3 and Q4**, indicating possible seasonal demand or successful marketing campaigns.

---

# 8. Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, several strategic recommendations can be made:

### Increase AOV

* Introduce **product bundling**
* Offer **cross-selling or upselling promotions**

### Maintain user acquisition strategy

The steady growth in active users indicates effective acquisition channels that should continue to be optimized.

### Analyze marketing campaigns

Investigate campaigns during **high-growth months (March, June, September)** to replicate successful strategies.

### Improve customer retention

Encourage repeat purchases through:

* loyalty programs
* targeted promotions
* personalized recommendations
